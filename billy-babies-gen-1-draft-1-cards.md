# Billy Babies — Gen 1, Draft 1 Card Sheet

**Version 0.1. Status: 50 slots assigned; 12 cards fully drafted; 38 cards at design-brief stage.** All gameplay assignments and numbers here are provisional. This is not yet a complete playable 50-card pool or a balance-tested set.

References: [design strategy](billy-babies-gen-1-draft-1-strategy.md), [approved Gen 1 roster](billy-babies-gen-1.md), [effect candidates](billy-babies-effects.md), [agreed rules](billy-babies-rules.md), and [prototype timing supplement v0.1](billy-babies-gen-1-draft-1-timing.md).

## The 50-slot plan

E = emotion shifting; D = discard and recovery; T = table building and sacrifice. “Other fit” identifies intended secondary uses to evaluate once the card is fully drafted; it does not certify that a deck is viable. Shared cards list their intended strategy fits in the same column.

C = Common; U = Uncommon; R = Rare; M = Mythic rare. Complexity describes the proposed effect, independently of rarity. “Drafted” means exact text and stats appear below. A “Brief” has neither final text nor assigned stats yet.

**Allocation:** 12 E + 12 D + 12 T + 14 shared = 50. Each primary group contains 4 enablers, 3 rewards, 3 flexible cards, and 2 answers/protection cards. Each contains 4 C, 5 U, 2 R, and 1 M; shared adds 4 C, 4 U, 5 R, and 1 M, yielding **16 C / 19 U / 11 R / 4 M** overall.

The 12 drafted cards are E01–E04, D01–D04, and T01–T04. They are interaction samples, not three four-card decks. No legal 20-card prototype deck is complete yet.

## Provisional numerical baseline

- **Shared quality pool:** brave, clever, curious, gentle, loyal, playful. These are proposed prototype qualities; the agreed rules do not yet name the pool. Use the normal random selection of three distinct qualities, revealing only the current round's quality.
- **Quality assignments:** exactly two distinct qualities per card for this version. Starting range: 3–6 points per listed quality. Only the matching Active Quality contributes; the two printed values never add together. The first 12 use 4–5 points and represent each quality on four cards.
- **Dice:** begin with 1–3 emotion dice per card. Most recurring engines start at one Normal die; flexible immediate scorers start around 6–8 expected dice points. This is a reference range, not a rigid formula or a rarity ladder. Normal averages 2.50, Strong 3.67, and Brutal 4.83 per die.
- **Base magic:** start within 30–60, independent of rarity. The first 12 use 35–50. At 40 magic per successful play, recharging 200 from zero takes five plays; at 50 it takes four, before other effects or adjustments.
- **Costs and rewards:** initial transfer limit 4 points; Paperoo's routine discard reward starts at 1 point, while specialized transfer and sacrifice watchers start at 2 points per qualifying turn; initial small-card removal threshold 40 adjusted magic. These are assumptions to test in combination, not established equivalent costs.

The first 12 deliberately include low-immediate-scoring engines and straightforward scoring cards for comparison. The remaining slots will supply more dice profiles and alternative enablers. Numerical comparisons use unadjusted magic unless a scenario explicitly applies the existing fatigue/underuse rules.

## First 12 card drafts

N = Normal die; S = Strong die. Any emotion not listed has no dice. “Persistent” cards stay on the table even without a quality match. “Immediate” cards stay only if they contributed matching quality points; otherwise they go to discard in placement step 4. Every uncanceled play still gains its magic in step 5 if the round continues.

### E01 — Balancini: A Little Balance

- **Rarity / role:** Uncommon; transfer enabler.
- **Dice:** Joy 1N; Shy 1N. Expected dice points: 5.00.
- **Qualities:** clever 5; curious 4. **Base magic:** 45. **Text:** immediate.

> Choose two different emotions and an amount from 1 to 4. Move that many of your points from the first emotion to the second, or all available points if the first has fewer.

Declare both lanes and the amount before the wand window. Moving zero actual points activates no transfer watcher. The long-legged balancing bird supplies the visual connection.

**Partners:** Twirlina rewards the transfer; Bumbini can supply Joy; Blushimi can supply Love. Planned Duufin and Leemo provide alternative transfer actions so Twirlina does not depend on Balancini alone.

**Decision and tradeoff:** Play to change a close lane or unlock a transfer reward; discard when there is no useful source surplus. The effect creates no points by itself, can weaken the source lane, and offers less raw dice scoring than Bumbini.

### E02 — Twirlina: Keep It Moving

- **Rarity / role:** Uncommon; transfer reward.
- **Dice:** Peaceful 1N. Expected dice points: 2.50.
- **Qualities:** playful 4; clever 4. **Base magic:** 35. **Text:** persistent.

> Once per turn, after another friendly card's text moves at least 1 point between two of your emotion lanes, gain 2 points in the destination lane.

The destination is taken from the qualifying transfer, with no new choice. A gain is not itself a transfer. The spinning body connects to keeping points in motion. Adapted from SYN-04.

**Partners:** Balancini now; Duufin, Leemo, and Doodiri in the remaining briefs. Tucktail can protect Twirlina from one opposing removal.

**Decision and tradeoff:** Play early with transfers available; discard late if there will be no qualifying play. Two qualifying transfers add 4 points to its 2.5 expected initial dice points, but its table magic and quality contribution also matter. Purrsie can target it directly.

### E03 — Bumbini: A Little Sunshine

- **Rarity / role:** Common; flexible scoring and a transfer source.
- **Dice:** Joy 2N; Love 1N. Expected dice points: 7.50.
- **Qualities:** loyal 5; playful 5. **Base magic:** 50. **Text:** immediate.

> If you're behind in Joy, gain 2 Joy.

Check the condition after Bumbini's dice and quality points resolve. Its bright, round bee appearance supports the cheerful scoring role.

**Partners:** Balancini redistributes its Joy; Paperoo can reward normally discarding a card with Joy or Love after playing it; Bobbin can recover it through a shared emotion. If a quality match keeps Bumbini on the table, it also supports Cuddlump.

**Decision and tradeoff:** Play for an immediate source of points or keep as a future recovery target. Normally discarding it can enable Paperoo or stock Blushimi's Love condition. It has no recurring reward, and extra Joy can be wasted in a lane already comfortably won.

### E04 — Tucktail: Under My Blanket

- **Rarity / role:** Common; protection for smaller engines.
- **Dice:** Shy 1N; Peaceful 1N. Expected dice points: 5.00.
- **Qualities:** gentle 5; loyal 4. **Base magic:** 40. **Text:** persistent.

> The first time each round an opponent's card text would remove another friendly table card with magic 40 or less, remove this card instead.

This replacement is automatic and counts as Tucktail being removed by the opponent, not sacrificed. Use adjusted magic. Its blanket tail supplies the visual connection. The timing supplement limits redirection to one replacement per removal.

**Partners:** Twirlina, Paperoo, Sporeboo, and Fluffernut all qualify at baseline magic. The first three also remain below 40 at the maximum 5% underuse bonus; Fluffernut can rise above it.

**Decision and tradeoff:** Play when protecting a qualifying table card matters. Its removal loses its own magic and reverses any quality contribution, potentially ending the round before the saved engine can help. It cannot protect a card from wand cancellation and does not protect larger targets such as Balancini at baseline.

### D01 — Bobbin: Pick Up the Thread

- **Rarity / role:** Uncommon; recovery enabler.
- **Dice:** Love 1N; Crazy 1N. Expected dice points: 5.00.
- **Qualities:** clever 5; loyal 4. **Base magic:** 45. **Text:** immediate.

> Choose an emotion, a card in your hand with dice for it, and a card in your discard pile with dice for it. You may exchange those cards. If you do, gain 2 points in that emotion.

Choose the emotion, both cards, and whether to exchange before the wand window. Bobbin itself is already out of the hand and cannot be an exchange target. The exchange is neither a normal discard nor a draw. The yarn body connects to retrieving a previous thread. Adapted from SYN-07.

**Partners:** Bumbini, Blushimi, Fluffernut, and Cuddlump all offer Love exchange options. Paperoo can reward the later normal discard. Planned Scuppup rewards the recovery event itself.

**Decision and tradeoff:** Play when a previously discarded answer or scorer is worth giving up a current hand card. The exchange requires valid cards in both places. No exchange occurs if either target becomes invalid, and there is no bonus without a completed exchange.

### D02 — Paperoo: Matching Letters

- **Rarity / role:** Uncommon; normal-discard reward.
- **Dice:** Shy 1N. Expected dice points: 2.50.
- **Qualities:** curious 4; clever 4. **Base magic:** 35. **Text:** persistent.

> Once per turn, when you make your normal discard, if that card shares an emotion on its printed dice with the card you successfully played this turn, gain 1 Crazy.

Paperoo can reward its own turn's normal discard because it enters before that discard. It cannot reward an earlier event. Its stationery wings connect to matching the played and discarded cards. Adapted from SYN-05, with a smaller starting reward because normal-discard opportunities occur on most turns and can begin on the turn it enters.

**Partners:** Bumbini and Blushimi overlap in Love; Bobbin overlaps with Love and Crazy cards; Balancini overlaps with Joy and Shy cards. Tucktail can protect Paperoo.

**Decision and tradeoff:** Play before several likely matching discards; discard when the remaining hand cannot support it or the round is about to end. Extra cost discards, exchanges, cancellations, and cards discarded after resolution do not trigger it.

### D03 — Blushimi: Feelings Kept Close

- **Rarity / role:** Common; flexible scorer and discard-pile reward.
- **Dice:** Love 1S; Shy 1N. Expected dice points: 6.17.
- **Qualities:** gentle 5; loyal 5. **Base magic:** 50. **Text:** immediate.

> If at least 2 cards in your discard pile have Love dice, gain 2 Sad.

Count cards, not dice. Blushimi is resolving and is not yet in the discard pile. Its face-covering fins and heart-shaped tail connect to Love, Shy, and a small Sad outlet.

**Partners:** Normally discarded Bumbini, Bobbin, Fluffernut, or Cuddlump can help satisfy the condition. Bobbin can recover Blushimi, while Balancini can redistribute its Love points.

**Decision and tradeoff:** Play for immediate Love/Shy or a third lane when the discard condition is met. Recovering a Love card can switch the bonus off, and reshuffling the discard pile can remove it. It offers no persistent scoring engine.

### D04 — Purrsie: Empty the Purse

- **Rarity / role:** Rare; removal paid for from the hand.
- **Dice:** Shy 1N; Crazy 1N. Expected dice points: 5.00.
- **Qualities:** curious 4; brave 4. **Base magic:** 40. **Text:** immediate.

> You may discard a chosen card from your hand. If you do, remove a chosen opposing table card with magic 40 or less.

Declare the cost card, the opposing target, and whether to pay before the wand window. The cost is paid only during uncanceled text resolution. The purse-shaped body connects to spending something held in reserve.

**Partners:** A cost discard can stock Blushimi's Love condition or provide a future Bobbin recovery target. Planned Scuppup benefits when that card is later recovered. Paperoo does not reward this extra discard.

**Decision and tradeoff:** Play to break a small engine without sacrificing your own table. With a full three-card starting hand, playing Purrsie and paying its cost leaves one card for the normal discard, followed by three refill attempts. The extra draw demand can accelerate penalties. A valid protection replacement can save the original target after the cost has been paid.

### T01 — Fluffernut: Something Left to Grow

- **Rarity / role:** Common; useful sacrifice target.
- **Dice:** Love 1N; Sad 1N. Expected dice points: 5.00.
- **Qualities:** gentle 4; curious 5. **Base magic:** 40. **Text:** persistent.

> When this card is sacrificed, gain 3 Peaceful.

The departure reward keeps Fluffernut on the table after a normal play. Record it as the card leaves; it resolves only if the round continues. The acorn body connects to leaving something useful behind. Adapted from SYN-10.

**Partners:** Mantini can sacrifice it to remove an opposing card with magic up to 40. Sporeboo rewards the same sacrifice. Bobbin can recover Fluffernut through Love or Sad. Cuddlump benefits while it stays on the table.

**Decision and tradeoff:** Keep it for table magic and family support, or give those up for a departure reward and removal. An opponent removing it does not grant the reward. Reversing its quality contribution can end the round before it gives Peaceful points.

### T02 — Sporeboo: A Gentle Send-Off

- **Rarity / role:** Uncommon; sacrifice reward.
- **Dice:** Sad 1N. Expected dice points: 2.50.
- **Qualities:** gentle 4; brave 4. **Base magic:** 35. **Text:** persistent.

> Once per turn, after you sacrifice another friendly card, gain 1 Joy and 1 Sad.

Sporeboo must be present when the sacrifice occurs and remain on the table when its reward resolves. Its ghostly spores connect to a small benefit after a friendly departure. Adapted from SYN-09, with a smaller starting reward.

**Partners:** Mantini supplies the first sacrifice action; Fluffernut supplies a target with its own reward. Planned Nukkeli adds another sacrifice action, while Liliwen and Glowgrub add targets. Tucktail can protect Sporeboo from opposing removal.

**Decision and tradeoff:** Play before a planned sacrifice sequence; discard when there is no action or target available. It offers only one Normal die initially. Sacrificing Sporeboo itself does not activate its ability, and its reward waits until the action causing the sacrifice finishes.

### T03 — Cuddlump: Room for One More

- **Rarity / role:** Common; flexible family scorer.
- **Dice:** Love 1N; Peaceful 1N. Expected dice points: 5.00.
- **Qualities:** brave 5; playful 5. **Base magic:** 50. **Text:** immediate.

> Gain 1 Love for each friendly table card with Love dice, up to 3.

Cuddlump has not yet entered the table and cannot count itself. Its oversized hugging arms connect to a family reward.

**Partners:** Fluffernut always remains after a successful play. Bumbini and Bobbin can contribute when their quality matches keep them on the table. Planned Hiveling and Lilynook offer more persistent Love support. Balancini can move any resulting Love surplus.

**Decision and tradeoff:** Play after establishing a Love table, or normally discard it for Paperoo and later recovery. Sacrificing its supporting cards reduces its bonus. Without support it supplies only 5 expected dice points, and it has no ongoing text.

### T04 — Mantini: A Careful Cut

- **Rarity / role:** Common; removal through sacrifice.
- **Dice:** Crazy 1N; Peaceful 1N. Expected dice points: 5.00.
- **Qualities:** brave 5; playful 4. **Base magic:** 45. **Text:** immediate.

> You may sacrifice a chosen friendly table card. If you do, remove a chosen opposing table card whose magic is no greater than the sacrificed card's magic.

Declare both targets and whether to sacrifice before the wand window. Compare adjusted magic values. Mantini is still resolving and cannot sacrifice itself. Its folded mantis blades connect to choosing a careful trade.

**Partners:** Fluffernut converts the sacrifice into a Peaceful reward; Sporeboo adds Joy and Sad; Bobbin can later recover a sacrificed Love card. Planned Liliwen and Glowgrub provide other targets, and Nukkeli provides an alternative sacrifice action.

**Decision and tradeoff:** Play when an opposing table card is worth the friendly card surrendered. You lose your table magic and any ongoing benefit; quality reversal may end the round before the opposing removal. A protection replacement may remove a different opposing card than the original target.

## Walkthroughs checked against the draft text

These are manual resolution checks using stated board positions, not simulated matches or evidence of balance. Quality positions are chosen to avoid an endpoint unless stated. The timing supplement defines event queues and replacements.

### Transfer creates a second lane opportunity

Twirlina is on your table. Immediately before Balancini's text, your Joy is 10 and Shy is 2. You declared a transfer of 4 from Joy to Shy. Balancini makes them Joy 6 / Shy 6; after its normal placement and wand gain, Twirlina's pending reward makes Shy 8. If the opponent has Joy 5 / Shy 7, this preserves one lead and takes another. Without Twirlina, the transfer alone does not take Shy.

This checks that the reward follows an actual transfer and that a point gain does not retrigger itself.

### Recovery and the normal discard are different events

Paperoo is already on your table. Your hand is Bobbin, Blushimi, and Mantini; Bumbini is in your discard pile. Declare Love and exchange Blushimi for Bumbini. After Bobbin resolves, the hand is Bumbini and Mantini. The exchange grants 2 Love but no Paperoo reward. Normally discard Bumbini: it shares Love with Bobbin, so Paperoo grants 1 Crazy. Keep Mantini and make two normal refill attempts.

This checks that recovering a card does not grant an extra play, and an exchange is not a normal discard.

### Sacrifice pays for removal and creates bounded rewards

Sporeboo and Fluffernut are on your table. Play Mantini and declare Fluffernut (magic 40) and an opposing Twirlina (magic 35). Assuming no cancellation, protection, or endpoint: remove Fluffernut and reverse its quality contribution, remove Twirlina and reverse its contribution, then complete Mantini's placement and wand gain. The queued departure reward grants 3 Peaceful; Sporeboo grants 1 Joy and 1 Sad. Neither point gain creates another sacrifice event.

This is 5 text-generated points beyond Mantini's dice, plus opposing removal, at the cost of Fluffernut and its table magic. It is an early balance concern to playtest, not a claim that the exchange is fair.

### An endpoint interrupts the sacrifice sequence

The marker is 26 toward the opponent, and your Fluffernut previously contributed 4 toward you. You play Mantini in a gentle round, so Mantini has no matching quality movement. Sacrificing Fluffernut reverses its 4-point contribution and reaches the opponent's endpoint. The round ends immediately: no opposing removal, departure reward, Sporeboo reward, Mantini placement, or wand gain. Score the round normally; the endpoint itself does not decide the winner.

### Protection does not count as your sacrifice

Your opponent plays Purrsie, pays its extra discard, and targets your Paperoo. Your Tucktail replaces the removal. Tucktail leaves, its quality points reverse, and Paperoo stays. Sporeboo receives no sacrifice event. The opponent still takes their normal discard after Purrsie finishes, if the round continues.

If Tucktail's quality reversal reaches an endpoint, stop there instead of continuing the turn. If two protectors are eligible in a later version, use the timing supplement's deterministic single-replacement rule.

### Cancellation prevents setup costs and rewards

Canceling Bobbin prevents its exchange and bonus. Canceling Purrsie prevents its extra discard and removal. Canceling Mantini prevents the sacrifice and removal. The canceled card still goes to its owner's discard pile and replacement-play refill follows the agreed rules; Paperoo does not treat the canceled card as a normal discard.

## Design findings and remaining work

- The representative cards contain a working transfer/reward pair, a recovery/normal-discard interaction, and a sacrifice/target/reward chain on paper. Each has several planned alternatives in the remaining slots, but those alternatives still need exact text and stats.
- The first 12 include all six emotions on printed dice and four appearances of each prototype quality. That is coverage, not evidence of equally strong emotion or quality decks.
- The three small recurring rewards have 35 base magic and low initial dice output. The 40-magic removal threshold reaches them even at a 5% underuse bonus. Fluffernut and Tucktail sit on the threshold and need adjustment-sensitive checks.
- For the first 12, only Bobbin returns cards from discard to hand, and it cannot target itself. There is no immediate self-recovery loop in this subset. Multiple alternative recovery cards in the remaining slots could enable repeated cross-turn reuse and delay exhaustion; assess them together before finalizing those effects.
- No first-12 text directly moves the quality marker. Quality motion comes from printed matches and removal reversal. S13 and S14 remain briefs until their text and reversible movement are specified.
- Next: finish the remaining 24 strategy slots, then the 14 shared slots; verify candidate pools and legal deck alternatives; build the five prototype decks; and conduct full-match playtests. The current walkthroughs do not replace those steps.
